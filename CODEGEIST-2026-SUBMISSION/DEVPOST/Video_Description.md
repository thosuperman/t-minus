```
T-Minus: go-live mission control for Jira (Codegeist 2026)
```

**Description**

```
T-Minus is a Forge app that runs an ERP or finance go-live minute by minute inside Jira. After every action it recalculates the critical path, the projected finish and the margin to each deadline, and shows how long the team still has to decide on a rollback. A Rovo agent, the Situation Commander, briefs leads and executives from the same recorded data.

In this demo:
0:00 The problem: a cutover night run from a spreadsheet
0:22 T-Minus inside a Jira project
0:45 Starting the cutover and executing steps
1:04 Simulated clock (demo mode) fast-forwards two hours
1:13 The data migration overruns; every dependent step is pushed back
1:50 The rollback decision window, and a blocker from a second session
2:22 Rovo Situation Commander answers from live plan data
3:05 Go/no-go decision recorded with evidence
3:26 The same decision on the Jira issue
3:34 Summary

Recorded from the app installed in Jira. All data is synthetic. The plan clock is simulated and labelled in the app. Two waiting periods are shortened. Narration is a synthetic voice.

Homepage and installation link: https://tminus.hayaix.tech
Source: https://github.com/thosuperman/t-minus
```
